\chapter{SYSTEM ANALYSIS AND DESIGN}

\section{Existing System}
The current laptop market in Gulhaji Plaza operates entirely offline. Buyers
visit multiple shops physically, compare prices from memory, and rely on vendor
recommendations.

\section{Proposed System}
The proposed system is an AI-powered laptop recommendation and marketplace
platform that provides personalized recommendations, transparent PKR prices,
verified vendor badges, and direct WhatsApp contact.

\section{System Architecture}
The system follows a Tri-Stack Microservices Architecture as illustrated in
Figure~\ref{fig:architecture}.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.95\textwidth]{figure-3-01-architecture.png}
  \caption{System Architecture Diagram of the AI-Powered Laptop Recommendation
    and Marketplace}
  \label{fig:architecture}
\end{figure}

The architecture comprises 5 frontend microfrontends, 7 backend microservices,
and a PostgreSQL 14 database.

\section{Database Design}
Table~\ref{tab:entities} presents the database entities.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Database Entities and Purposes}
  \label{tab:entities}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}lp{9cm}@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Table} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
    \midrule
    users          & Authentication for all roles \\
    vendors        & Vendor profile linked to a user \\
    shops          & Physical shop locations \\
    laptops        & Static laptop model data \\
    specifications & Hardware specs (1:1 with laptop) \\
    inventory      & Vendor offerings (price, stock) \\
    price\_history & Audit log of price changes \\
    reviews        & User reviews for laptops \\
    notifications  & Message log for users \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
